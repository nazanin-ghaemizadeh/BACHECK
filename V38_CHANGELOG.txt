V38 — English table accent alignment and English-only export filenames

- In English mode, the colored criterion accent line in the management summary table is aligned to the left edge.
- All generated/downloaded filenames are now English/ASCII-safe regardless of the active interface language.
- Evaluator JSON: vehicle_assessment_<model>.json
- Evaluator Excel: vehicle_assessment_<model>.xlsx
- Expert Excel: criterion_weights_<expert>.xlsx
- Manager Excel: management_dashboard_<model>.xlsx
- Full case backup: full_case_backup_<model>_<date>.json
- Non-Latin model/person names fall back to vehicle/expert in filenames to keep filenames English-only.
