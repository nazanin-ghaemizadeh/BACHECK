V39 — Management-only edit authorization

- Reopen/Edit Authorization is now visible only in Management, never in Evaluator or Expert.
- Role redraws can no longer accidentally reveal the management unlock button.
- Entering editor name and revision reason in Management immediately creates a visible revision-session record.
- The same revision record is updated as actual edits occur, instead of waiting for the first edit or creating repeated short-window entries.
- Persian and English revision-history date/time labels distinguish authorization from applied revision.
